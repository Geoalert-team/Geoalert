# Newsfeed Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A public newsfeed of DRRMO/BFP announcements and new-alert posts, on a News page and as "Latest updates" on the home page, with posting, editing and deleting for DRRMO, admins and BFP.

**Architecture:** A new Django app `apps/news` stores only announcements. The feed endpoint builds alert posts on the fly from hazard zones that have at least one `HazardAlert`, merges them with announcements by time, and pages with a `before` timestamp. React gets an API module, a card component, a News page with a posting form, and a home-page section.

**Tech Stack:** Django 4.2 + DRF 3.15 + PostGIS (Supabase), React 18 (CRA), Jest via `react-scripts test`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-newsfeed-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Title: required after trimming, at most **150** characters. Text: required after trimming, at most **5,000** characters.
- Photo: optional; a real image (Pillow-verified, as report photos), extension **jpg, jpeg, png or webp**, at most **5 MB** (reuse `apps.reports.serializers.validate_photo_size`).
- Photo storage key: `news/YYYY/MM/<post uuid><ext>`; URL through `apps.reports.serializers.photo_url(image, request)`.
- `source` is `DRRMO` or `BFP`: `BFP` when the author's role is `BFP_Personnel`, else `DRRMO` (admins post as DRRMO).
- Post: roles `DRRMO_Officer`, `System_Admin`, `BFP_Personnel` (`utils.permissions.IsBFPPersonnel`). Edit/delete: the author, or any `DRRMO_Officer` / `System_Admin`.
- Feed: `limit` default **10**, max **30**; newest first; `next_before` null at the end.
- Alert post exists for a zone with at least one `HazardAlert`, not `is_sample`, status not `Retracted`; `published_at` = earliest alert's `created_at`; `status` `active` for Active/Resolving, `resolved` for Resolved/Archived; `map_link` `/map?zone=<id>` only while active.
- Audit actions: `POST_NEWS`, `EDIT_NEWS`, `DELETE_NEWS` (table `announcement`).
- Post text renders as text with its line breaks, never HTML. Menu label **News**, between Map and About. Home section title **Latest updates**, 3 posts, link **See all news**.
- The public sees the office (DRRMO/BFP), never an author's name.
- Table `announcement`, columns prefixed `anno_`.

## Review Focus

1. A bad `before` (not a date) or `limit` (not a number) answers 400 with a message, and `limit` over 30 is cut to 30 — never a 500. *(Task 2)*
2. A zone raised to Extreme after it went out has two alerts but is **one** post, dated by the first alert, showing the current level. *(Task 2)*
3. A post whose author's account was deleted still shows with its office, and only DRRMO/admins can edit it. *(Task 2)*
4. Editing only the title keeps the existing photo. *(Task 1)*
5. A title or text containing HTML (`<b>x</b>`) shows literally, with no element made from it. *(Task 3)*

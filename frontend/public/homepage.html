What the colors mean

The map uses the same three colors everywhere. Here is what each one asks of you.

  1. Low risk

    The area is safe. Standard monitoring is in place.

    You: go about your day and keep your go-bag ready.

  2. Medium risk

    Conditions could get worse. Stay alert.

    You: watch weather updates and know your evacuation route.

  3. High risk

    Immediate action is required.

    You: prepare to evacuate and follow barangay officials.
